# Claims on the public pages

MAINT 7.4, step 4. For each claim: where it is, the evidence found, and proposed words (or "remove").
This is not legal advice; the owner decides. "Both designs" means today's component and the new design's
page, both of which carry the same words (rebranding D4.14 kept today's words), so each change is made in
both until R.2 deletes today's. The new design's pages are `app/support-us/NewSupportUs.tsx`,
`app/how-it-works/NewHowItWorks.tsx`, `app/for-coordinators/NewForCoordinators.tsx`,
`app/NewHomePage.tsx` and `app/faqs/NewFaqs.tsx`.

Verdicts: **Keep** (evidence found), **Reword** (partly true), **Remove** (nothing supports it, or the evidence
contradicts it), **Owner** (only the owner knows, or it is a legal conclusion).

**Decided by the owner on 23 October 2026.** IB Match has no registered company; it is a hobby project run by an
individual (Pavel Yampolsky, Poland), with **no donations received so far and paying customers (schools)**. The owner
accepted every recommendation in this table, and answered the open ones: free "forever" becomes "free for students; we
have no plans to charge them"; Tal is the founder and it was built together with Pavel; the early adopter program is
open; **the four coordinator quotes are not real**; the 24–48-hour reply time is true; `privacy@` and `legal@` forward to
`support@`. So: "non-profit" and "non-commercial" go (schools pay); the whole "where your support goes" section goes (it
allocates money that has not been received); "Most Popular", "100%" and the "covers…" lines go; "GDPR Compliant" goes;
"The only…" goes; the coordinator quotes go; the Ko-fi page is edited to match. **Every row now has a decision.** Rows
marked **Decided** are settled; the rest keep their original proposal, which the owner accepted.
